About Us:

🌸 Welcome to the UC Taiwanese Club! 🌸
We are a warm and friendly community dedicated to sharing and celebrating Taiwanese culture, food, and traditions. Our goal is to create a space where students can connect, learn, and have fun together. Whether you're from Taiwan, have Taiwanese friends, or are simply curious about the culture, everyone is welcome to join!
